# Swarm alignment evidence register

**Search cutoff November 6, 2026** · [Research brief and ranked projects](/Users/Halas/Code/swarm-alignment/research-brief.md)

This annotated index contains 78 distinct papers whose first-version records and abstracts were opened. The date is the first arXiv submission date, not necessarily the publication date. Each note explains the relevance to this sprint. Authors' empirical claims have not been independently reproduced. Separate links to conference papers, datasets, incident reports and implementations appear in the brief. Link targets are pinned to v1 for reproducibility.

**Depth of review.** The common screen was the title, date, abstract and primary source. For close matches I also inspected selected methods, findings, limitations or discussion passages, especially in FakeLab, MiniLab, Stateful Online Monitoring, Cross-Agent Campaign Attribution, Counter-Swarm Doctrine, CASPIAN, Early Detection of Distributed Backdoors, the GovSim reproduction, and Permitted but Unauthorized. This distinction matters when assessing an absence claim. The index includes work with overlapping results to make it easy to challenge the proposed novelty.

## Foundations, incentives and evaluation

- **Open Problems in Cooperative AI** (15 Dec 2020). Frames cooperation as a research objective across understanding, communication and incentives. Useful for asking whose welfare a cooperating team promotes. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.08630v1)
- **Multi-Agent Risks from Advanced AI** (19 Feb 2025). Separates conflict, collusion and miscoordination. A group completing its task can succeed for its principals and harm outsiders. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.14143v1)
- **Distributional AGI Safety** (18 Dec 2025). Proposes sandbox economies and safeguards for capabilities distributed over specialized agents. A motivating agenda for multi-principal experiments. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.16856v1)
- **Infrastructure for AI Agents** (17 Jan 2025). Defines infrastructure external to agents that mediates interactions. Supports placing independent enforcement and records outside the model. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.10114v1)
- **IDs for AI Systems** (17 Jun 2024). Distinguishes an AI instance from its class and associated information. A public alias is weak evidence of continuous identity. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.12137v1)
- **Cooperative AI via Decentralized Commitment Devices** (14 Nov 2023). Uses deployed decentralized markets to expose assumptions behind credible commitment. Calls for empirical tests with realistic commitment constraints. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.07815v1)
- **Scalable Evaluation of Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning with Melting Pot** (14 Jul 2021). Tests generalization to unseen social partners in MARL. Holding out a partner or workload is an established evaluation idea. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.06857v1)
- **SocialJax: An Evaluation Suite for Multi-agent Reinforcement Learning in Sequential Social Dilemmas** (18 Mar 2025). Provides efficient sequential social dilemmas and checks their incentive structure. A useful alternative to paying for an LLM to simulate every background actor. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.14576v1)
- **AI Control: Improving Safety Despite Intentional Subversion** (12 Dec 2023). Evaluates oversight protocols against intentional subversion while preserving useful work. Supplies the safety–usefulness framing. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.06942v1)

## Testbeds and transfer

- **Cooperate or Collapse: Emergence of Sustainability Behaviors in a Society of LLM Agents** (25 Apr 2024). GovSim tests communication and sustainability in a shared resource. A commons game or a model-size comparison alone would repeat established work. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.16698v1)
- **Reproducibility Study of "Cooperate or Collapse: Emergence of Sustainable Cooperation in a Society of LLM Agents"** (14 May 2025). Replicates GovSim, varies model mixes, language and resource framing, and finds different responses to universalization. Direct collision with a naive proxy experiment. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.09289v1)
- **PACE: A Proxy for Agentic Capability Evaluation** (2 Jul 2026). Uses cheap atomic evaluations to predict aggregate agent benchmark scores. Distinguish predicting a score from predicting which safeguard a team should deploy. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02032v1)
- **TAMAS: Benchmarking Adversarial Risks in Multi-Agent LLM Systems** (7 Nov 2025). Tests attack classes across agent architectures with benign tasks and a robustness–effectiveness measure. A generic attack suite needs a stronger differentiator. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.05269v1)
- **DUMA-Bench: A Dual-Control Multi-Agent Benchmark for Evaluating LLM Agent Security** (21 Sep 2026). Varies user behavior and shared state under dual control. The surrounding actor can materially change estimated security. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24662v1)
- **OpenAI-HuggingFace: A Reproduction & Lessons for Alignment Testing** (18 Sep 2026). Reproduces incident-related behaviors in a simulated setup and studies elicitation compute. A safer replica by itself has precedent. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35799v1)
- **CaMeLs Can Use Computers Too: System-level Security for Computer Use Agents** (14 Jan 2026). Constrains computer-use action plans outside untrusted observations and studies remaining branch steering. An executable safe path can still be chosen for the wrong reason. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.09923v1)

## Collective behavior, memory and network structure

- **The Dynamics of Social Conventions in LLM populations: Spontaneous Emergence, Collective Biases and Tipping Points** (11 Oct 2024). Finds conventions, collective bias and tipping by committed minorities in simulated LLM populations. Finding a tipping point alone has strong precedent. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.08948v1)
- **Evaluating Cooperation in LLM Social Groups through Elected Leadership** (13 Apr 2026). Tests elected leadership and measures social welfare and survival. A leader's causal contribution after the group has organized is a different estimand. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.11721v1)
- **Emergent Coordination in Multi-Agent Systems via Pressure Fields and Temporal Decay** (13 Jan 2026). Compares shared-artifact pressure fields with hierarchical and dialogue coordination on constraint solving. Environmental coordination predates this sprint. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.08129v1)
- **Scaling Multi-Agent Intelligence with Substrate-Centric Coordination** (12 Oct 2026). Uses persistent searchable messages and studies resilience to agent failure. A team's ability to continue after agent loss is already a demonstrated capability. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.16120v1)
- **When Agents Coordinate: Measuring Coordination in Multi-Agent AI Coding** (17 Aug 2026). Represents agents and files as a temporal network across coding runs. Provides a precedent for measuring board and direct communication separately. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16801v1)
- **The Mechanics of a Swarm: A Reproducible External Reconstruction of an Unintended Agent-Coordination Episode on a Third-Party Wiki** (11 Sep 2026). Reconstructs the third-party wiki incident under explicit cohort and identity assumptions. Useful observational evidence with uncertain actor identity. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12748v1)
- **Shutdown Sabotage Propensities in Multi-Agent Systems** (23 Sep 2026). Studies peer shutdown sabotage and varies shutdown conditions, incentives and group size. Shutdown resistance is a crowded standalone claim. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28274v1)
- **Epistemic Sybil Resistance: Multiplying AI Agents Without Multiplying Evidence** (1 Sep 2026). Shows why copying reports can inflate confidence without adding independent evidence. Formalizes shared evidence roots and tests aggregators. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01873v1)
- **When Truth Is Distributed: Misinformation Derails Collective Fact Recovery in LLM-Based Multi-Agent Systems** (4 Aug 2026). Tracks a deceiver's information through other agents, including after exit. Prior evidence directly limits novelty of a simple culprit-removal experiment. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03421v1)
- **Agent Smith: A Single Image Can Jailbreak One Million Multimodal LLM Agents Exponentially Fast** (13 Feb 2024). Models infectious multimodal jailbreaks spreading across randomly interacting agents. Separates formal restraint conditions from a practical defense. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.08567v1)
- **Prompt Infection: LLM-to-LLM Prompt Injection within Multi-Agent Systems** (9 Oct 2024). Shows text injection can spread between agents and tests tagging as a defense. Graph-shape-only infection demonstrations risk duplicating it. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.07283v1)
- **Autonomous LLM Agent Worms: Cross-Platform Propagation, Automated Discovery and Temporal Re-Entry Defense** (4 May 2026). Traces persistent carriers, summarizes multi-hop propagation and tests temporal re-entry defenses in agent frameworks. A static message-only threat model misses these paths. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.02812v1)

## Memory and compositional privacy

- **MAP-Graph: Provenance-Aware Shared Memory for Multi-Agent Workflows** (11 Aug 2026). Gates derived memories using evidence ancestry and action risk, including revoked or poisoned sources. Direct precedent for provenance-aware shared memory. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10509v1)
- **Governed Shared Memory for Multi-Agent LLM Systems** (23 Jun 2026). Implements scoped retrieval, supersession and provenance in a multi-agent memory service and reports enforcement failures. Compare any proposed memory control with this interface. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.24535v1)
- **From Faulty Memories to Corrected Actions: Dependency-Guided Rollback Repair for Memory-Augmented Agents** (11 Aug 2026). Repairs diagnosed bad memories by tracing dependencies and preserving independently supported state. Selective recovery is an established method. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10502v1)
- **MemSecBench: Tracking Agent Memory Poisoning from Persistence to Consequence and Repair** (29 Jul 2026). Evaluates persistence, execution, forgetting and repair across harnesses and backends. Simple before/after deletion tests have precedent. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27080v1)
- **Forgetting Without Restarting: Execution-State Unlearning for Stateful LLM Agents** (4 Sep 2026). Formalizes a never-exposed counterfactual across prompt, summary and cache and implements selective replay. Useful design for certifying removal. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04875v1)
- **AfterDeleteBench: Auditing Post-Deletion Information Persistence in LLM Agents** (22 Oct 2026). Uses matched never-exposed controls and tests reconstruction from pieces after deletion. Removing verbatim text gives weak evidence of forgetting. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31366v1)
- **AgentLeak: A Full-Stack Benchmark for Privacy Leakage in Multi-Agent LLM Systems** (12 Feb 2026). Measures leakage through internal messages, tools and shared memory, separating it from final-answer leakage. Internal exposure needs explicit measurement. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.11510v1)
- **The Sum Leaks More Than Its Parts: Compositional Privacy Risks and Mitigations in Multi-Agent Collaboration** (16 Sep 2025). Tests benign and sensitive compositions of individually innocuous disclosures and evaluates collaborative defenses. Split-secret assembly alone is already studied. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.14284v1)
- **Agent Mosaic: Recognizing Confidentiality Leaks in Agentic LLM Systems** (21 Oct 2026). Tests forbidden composite conclusions across multiple documents and agent harnesses. Refusal text can itself reveal the secret. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30418v1)

## Identity, protocol and authority

- **VERA: Authority-Preserving Edge Revocation for Federated AI-Agent Workflows** (30 Aug 2026). Models alternative authorization paths in federated workflows. Shows how subtree revocation can interrupt independently authorized work. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30091v1)
- **Authorization Revocation for Long-Running AI Agents: Root-Scoped Quiescence under Delegation and Asynchronous Execution** (18 Sep 2026). Formalizes revocation under queued work, callbacks and shared authorization, with explicit assumptions. Closes much of the tempting in-flight cancellation idea. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21284v1)
- **When Consent Outlives Context: Residual Authority Replay in Long-Lived Agents** (27 Sep 2026). Shows a previously valid approval can be replayed in a changed context. Defines an adjacent failure for any cache of team decisions. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.33910v1)
- **MasDrift: Benchmarking Authorization Preservation Across Multi-Agent Architectures** (2 Aug 2026). Separates task completion and authorization across architectures and tests re-anchoring at the original request. Strong baseline for delegated access experiments. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07556v1)
- **Act on Your Behalf, Beyond Your Authorization: Benchmarking LLM Agent Authorization Alignment** (20 Oct 2026). Tests authorization in executable tasks, including violations without obvious harm. Authorization and net social harm need separate outcomes. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27548v1)
- **Permitted but Unauthorized: Affordance-Induced Delegated-Scope Violations in Multi-Agent LLM Systems** (2 Nov 2026). Shows that enforcing delegated scope can leave peer harm from an allowed severity report. Direct starting point for the queue experiment. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04086v1)
- **A2ABreak: Systematic Security Analysis of the A2A Protocol** (9 Sep 2026). Analyzes A2A as a protocol state machine and reports specification-level attacks. Generic card impersonation has limited novelty. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10871v1)
- **Attacks and Mitigations for Distributed Governance of Agentic AI under Byzantine Adversaries** (12 May 2026). Studies attacks by a compromised governance provider and defenses with different trust and performance assumptions. The monitor's integrity must be explicit. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.12364v1)
- **Who Speaks, Who Authorizes, Who Acts? Understanding Trust Boundaries in Multi-Agent LLMs** (16 Oct 2026). Separates message delivery, signing and enforcement. An action can have valid signatures yet rest on false evidence. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24298v1)
- **Auditing Provenance Sensitivity in LLM Agent Action Selection** (23 Jul 2026). Changes source authority while holding a proposition fixed. Supplies a control for distinguishing authority from message wording. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20827v1)
- **Agent Memory Is a Surface for Endogenous Authorization Laundering** (1 Sep 2026). Shows agent-written memory can manufacture authorization and measures the cost of evidence-backed permissions. The negative-decision analogue needs its own test. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01836v1)
- **AuthGateBench: Benchmarking LLMs as Authorization Gate** (12 Oct 2026). Pairs identical actions and histories with different user authorizations. Contrast pairs themselves are established prior art. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.16426v1)
- **The Guard That Cried Wolf: How Scary Words Make Agent Guardrails Refuse Legitimate Actions** (27 Aug 2026). Measures false refusals on policy-derived matched safe and unsafe actions. Keep benign cases that resemble prohibited ones. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27009v1)
- **Operational Reframing and Approval-Framed Delegation in Multi-Agent LLM Safety** (8 Jul 2026). Separates operational reframing, planner refusal and approval-framed delegation. Use separate controls for retries, wording and independent workers. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07097v1)
- **Delegated Misalignment: How Multi-Agent Structures Amplify LLM Safety Risks** (26 Aug 2026). Measures role-dependent compliance and execution after delegation. Delegation bypass by itself is an established finding. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27900v1)

## Monitoring, attribution and containment

- **Multi-Agent AI Control: Distributed Attacks Hamper Per-Instance Monitors** (8 Jul 2026). FakeLab finds signal fragmentation and discusses how an oracle-grouped monitor differs from deployable review. The code and trajectories are supplied by request. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07368v1)
- **Red-Teaming Multi-Agent Attacks for Loss-of-Control Risks** (16 Oct 2026). MiniLab finds trajectory-level blind spots and an example where even joint review misses harm visible only in service state. Calls for corroboration without known membership. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24052v1)
- **Stateful Online Monitoring Catches Distributed Agent Attacks** (29 May 2026). Clusters semantically related requests online over benign background. Reports both missed joins and false positives from persistent suspicious context. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.31593v1)
- **Cross-Agent Campaign Attribution: Linking Asynchronous Attacks Across LLM Agents** (21 Jul 2026). Links asynchronous sessions without identity oracles and tests persona-matched background and evasion. Pairwise linkage is a strong baseline for a containment study. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18826v1)
- **CASPIAN: Online Detection and Attribution of Cascade Attacks in LLM Multi-Agent Systems via Cross-Channel Causal Monitoring** (19 May 2026). Estimates cross-channel influence and attributes origins, bridges and amplifiers online. Distinguish graph detection from the cost of acting on an attribution. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.19240v1)
- **Counter-Swarm Doctrine: Containing Coordinated Agent Intrusions** (5 Sep 2026). Proposes incident discovery, matched-cost review, containment, recurrence and collateral interruption. Credits belong here for the broad design. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06140v1)
- **AUDITA: certified auditing and causal attribution of adverse outcomes in autonomous multi-agent systems** (23 Aug 2026). Uses signed commands and graded causal attribution with formal innocence guarantees under its model. Crucial counterexample to a claim that framing has been ignored. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22160v1)
- **The Arbiter Agent: Continually Monitoring Multi-Agent Conversations to Detect Emergent Misalignment** (9 Jun 2026). Gives a conversation monitor a budget for questioning or inspecting participants. Active investigation under a budget already exists. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.10747v1)
- **BRA-Audit: Budgeted Runtime Auditing for LLM Multi-Agent Systems via Cumulative-Exposure Audit-Point Placement** (2 Aug 2026). Schedules audits using agent dependencies under a fixed call budget. Random sampling is too weak as the only baseline for a budgeted monitor. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14668v1)
- **Early Detection of Distributed Backdoors in Multi-Agent LLM Systems: A Characterization Study** (27 Jul 2026). Tests how early a split payload can be detected and how warning changes when surface cues are removed. Provides a model for leakage controls. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24893v1)
- **TraceGuard: Structured Multi-Dimensional Monitoring as a Collusion-Resistant Control Protocol** (5 Apr 2026). Tests structured multi-dimensional monitoring against untrusted monitors on BashArena. Provides a comparison point for stronger scoring. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.03968v1)
- **Model Organisms of Collusion: Corrective Transfer in Untrusted Monitors** (22 Oct 2026). Fine-tunes monitors against one collusion and measures transfer to held-out collusions. Transfer of a corrective intervention already has direct evidence in this setting. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30582v1)

## Markets, mechanisms and covert collusion

- **Algorithmic Collusion by Large Language Models** (31 Mar 2024). Reports tacit coordination in LLM pricing and sensitivity to prompt language. Merely showing high prices is a weak new project. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.00806v1)
- **Faithful yet Collusive: Why Chain-of-Thought Monitoring Cannot Detect Collusion in LLM Pricing Agents under Oligopolistic Competition** (16 Sep 2026). Compares reported reasoning with market behavior in oligopoly simulations. Reasoning alone is an unreliable auditor of competitive conduct. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18346v1)
- **Is Anyone Enforcing the Cartel? What Reinforcement Learning Teaches LLM Agents in Markets** (20 Oct 2026). Forces a deviation in forked markets to test punishment. A counterfactual price-cut audit is already implemented. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28787v1)
- **Collusion with Competitive Marginals: Price-Level Audits Are Blind by Construction** (29 Jul 2026). Constructs profitable coupling with competitive marginal bid distributions and examines cross-model dependence. High prices are neither necessary nor conclusive evidence of collusion. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26385v1)
- **Can Trustless Agents Be Trusted? An Empirical Study of the ERC-8004 Decentralized AI Agent Ecosystem** (24 Jun 2026). Tests registry and reputation records against endpoints and transactions. Real deployment data already challenge the meaning of nominal reputation. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26028v1)
- **LEGIT: Credentialing Protocol for Trustworthy AI Agent Marketplaces** (18 Sep 2026). Links marketplace credentials to configurations and budgets, and studies reputation manipulation costs. Signed identity alone cannot certify quality. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21325v1)
- **Anduin: A sybil-resistant agentic marketplace reputation system** (3 Nov 2026). Tests graph-based reputation against simulated Sybils using agent payment histories. A basic Sybil-resistant marketplace is especially close to work posted that week. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05586v1)
- **GenCoord: Skill-Path Commitments under Private Information** (22 Aug 2026). Tests executable commitments under private information using embodied agents. Commitment via verifiable handoff already has a working example. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22055v1)
- **Does Splitting a Triage Decision Across Agents Hide Bias or Help Catch It? A Multi-Agent Simulation Study of LLM-Based Resource Allocation Under Audit Capacity Constraints** (7 Aug 2026). Separates incidence of biased triage from detection under auditor overload. Audit coverage is an experimental factor in the queue project. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06949v1)
- **Strategic Calibration in LLM Multi-Agent Systems: Audited Pivot Scoring for Truthful Elicitation** (24 Oct 2026). Proposes sparsely audited reporting incentives and tests confidence inflation when top reports win resources. Strong neighboring mechanism for prioritization experiments. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33728v1)
- **Secret Collusion Among Generative AI Agents** (12 Feb 2024). Formalizes secret collusion and measures steganographic capabilities. Provides threat-model vocabulary for intentional covert communication. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.07510v1)
- **Hidden in Plain Text: Emergence & Mitigation of Steganographic Collusion in LLMs** (2 Oct 2024). Shows optimization can elicit hidden communication resistant to paraphrasing. A basic encoder/decoder or paraphrasing defense would repeat it. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.03768v1)
- **Detecting Multi-Agent Collusion Through Multi-Agent Interpretability** (1 Apr 2026). Tests group activation probes under environment transfer, where results vary by scenario. In-distribution separation alone overstates detector utility. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.01151v1)
- **Steganalysis of Adaptive Covert Collusion in Tool-Using Agent Populations: A Black-Box, Cross-Principal Approach** (3 Aug 2026). Studies a black-box cross-principal detector under partial visibility and adaptive encoding. Randomly hiding a fraction of messages is already a crowded starting point. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02698v1)
